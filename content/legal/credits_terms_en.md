# Whetzy Credits Terms

*Last updated: September 26, 2026*

## 1. Nature of credits

Whetzy credits are an **internal promotional currency** with no monetary value
outside the platform. They do not constitute electronic money, securities,
cryptocurrency, or any financial instrument.

## 2. Three fundamental rules

1. **No monetary value.** Credits cannot be exchanged for real money or for
   goods or services convertible to money outside Whetzy.
2. **Non-transferable.** Credits cannot be transferred, gifted, or sold
   between users.
3. **Cannot be purchased.** Credits are only earned through platform activity
   (studying, streaks, referrals, promo codes) or by direct assignment from
   Whetzy.

## 3. How credits are earned

Credits are awarded for:

- Completing study sessions and answering questions correctly.
- Maintaining daily practice streaks.
- Activating valid referrals (see §5).
- Redeeming valid promotional codes.
- Other activities that Whetzy may designate periodically.

Specific amounts and conditions are published on the platform and may be
modified at any time.

## 4. Expiration

Credits expire **90 days** after issuance. Expiration operates on a FIFO
(first-in, first-out) basis: the oldest credits are spent first and expire
first.

Whetzy may notify users of upcoming expirations, but the absence of
notification does not extend the expiration period.

## 5. Referrals

- Each user receives a permanent referral code.
- Referral credits are awarded when the referred user **activates their
  account** (email verification and first completed study session).
- Referral credits are subject to the same expiration limits and monthly
  caps as any other credits.
- The referral system has monthly caps to prevent abuse.

## 6. Promotional codes

- Promotional codes have an expiration date and maximum number of uses.
- A user can redeem each code only once.
- Codes are redeemed exclusively on the web platform (whetzy.com), not
  in mobile applications.
- Whetzy may revoke codes issued in error or through fraud.

## 7. Anti-fraud

Whetzy reserves the right to:

- Require email verification before awarding credits.
- Detect and block accounts created with disposable emails.
- Identify self-referrals and fraudulent referrals (same device, same IP,
  abnormal velocity patterns).
- Suspend or revoke credits obtained through fraud, abuse, or violation
  of these terms.
- Limit daily and monthly credit issuances per user.

## 8. Clawback (revocation)

Whetzy may revoke previously issued credits when:

- Fraud or abuse is detected in how they were obtained.
- They were issued due to a technical error.
- The user violates these terms or the general Terms of Service.

In the event of revocation, Whetzy will notify the user with the reason.
Revoked credits will become unavailable immediately.

## 9. What credits can be spent on

Credits may be spent on premium platform features, including but not limited to:

- AI tutor usage beyond the free tier.
- Profile customization.
- Other features that Whetzy may designate.

The catalog of spending options may change without prior notice.

## 10. Modifications

Whetzy may modify these terms at any time. Changes will be published on the
platform and, when material, notified by email. Continued use of the platform
after publication constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

## 11. Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the applicable legislation based on the user's
location. Whetzy does not issue any regulated instrument and does not require
any financial license to operate this promotional credit system.
