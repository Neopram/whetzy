# Privacy Policy — Learning Engine

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address, display name, and profile avatar
- Authentication credentials (hashed, never stored in plain text)

### Learning Data
- Quiz responses, scores, and timestamps
- Skill mastery levels and progress
- Spaced repetition scheduling data (FSRS parameters)
- Streak and XP history

### Device Information
- Device type, OS version, and app version
- Language and timezone preferences
- Push notification tokens (if enabled)

### Usage Analytics
- Session duration and frequency
- Feature interaction patterns (anonymized)
- Crash reports and performance metrics

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Adaptive Learning:** Your quiz responses and accuracy power our FSRS, Elo, and BKT algorithms to personalize question difficulty, review scheduling, and learning path recommendations.
- **Gamification:** Streak tracking, XP, leagues, and achievements are calculated from your learning activity.
- **Service Improvement:** Anonymized, aggregated usage data helps us improve question quality and learning outcomes.
- **Communications:** Email notifications about account security, streak reminders (if opted in), and product updates.

## 3. AI & Machine Learning

Our adaptive engine uses machine learning models (FSRS-6, Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, Deep Knowledge Tracing) to personalize your learning experience. These models:
- Run locally on your device for real-time adaptation
- Are trained on anonymized, aggregated data only
- Do not make automated decisions that affect your rights
- Can be reset at any time from your profile settings

Per Apple Guideline 5.1.2(i), we disclose that your learning data is processed by AI to generate personalized recommendations. No personal data is shared with third-party AI providers.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share data only:
- With infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics) under strict data processing agreements
- When required by law or valid legal process
- With your explicit consent

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- Active accounts: data retained while account is active
- Account deletion: all personal data deleted within 30 days
- Anonymized learning analytics may be retained indefinitely
- Request deletion at any time via Settings > Delete Account or by emailing support

## 6. Children's Privacy

Learning Engine is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly.

## 7. Security

- All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- OAuth 2.0 / JWT authentication

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Export your data in a portable format
- Opt out of analytics

## 9. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in servers located in the United States and European Union, in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification and email at least 30 days before they take effect.

## 11. Contact

For privacy inquiries: privacy@learningengine.app
