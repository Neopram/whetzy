## Objective
Introduce Whetzy as an adaptive learning platform in 2 minutes. Target: career-switchers, students, tech professionals.

## Structure

### Intro (0:00-0:15)
- **Visual:** Quiz screen on mobile, user answering a Python question
- **Narration:** "Tired of one-size-fits-all courses that waste your time? Whetzy is the engine that learns *while* you learn."
- **SFX:** Smooth whoosh, minimalist tone

### Problem (0:15-0:35)
- **Visual:** Quick montage: Generic competitor, slow competitor, surface-level competitor
- **Narration:** "Most platforms teach everyone the same way. They bore you with what you know and skip over what you need."
- **Stat on-screen:** "47% dropout in first month"

### Solution (0:35-1:20)
- **Visual:** Whetzy dashboard, skill mastery map (territory), active streak
- **Narration:** "Whetzy uses adaptive learning powered by Bayesian Knowledge Tracing. Every question calibrates to *your* exact level."

- **Visual:** Credit wallet, achievement unlocked, duel against another learner
- **Narration:** "Real gamification: streaks, turn-based duels against other learners, daily missions. Learning feels like a game that matters."

- **Visual:** Verifiable certificate, QR code, LinkedIn share
- **Narration:** "Master a track, get a certificate verified by Whetzy. Prove to employers you didn't just watch videos."

### Call-to-Action (1:20-2:00)
- **Visual:** Whetzy landing page, 'Start Free' button
- **Narration:** "Start today. Free. No credit card. In 60 seconds you're answering real questions."
- **Overlay:** whetzy.com + campaign code YT-EN-PROD
- **SFX:** Positive tone, fade out with subtle music
