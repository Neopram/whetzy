## Issue: Can't change language

**Subject:** Re: How to change my account language

Hi {name},

To change your Whetzy account language:

1. Open your profile (user icon at the bottom)
2. Look for "Language"
3. Select Spanish (ES) or English (EN)

The change applies immediately across the entire platform.

If something doesn't work, let us know more details and we'll be happy to help.

Thanks!
Whetzy Team
