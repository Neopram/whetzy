## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 User-Provided Information

When you create an account or use our Service, we may collect:

- **Account Data**: Name, email address, password, profile picture, phone number (optional), date of birth (optional)
- **Profile Information**: Learning preferences, educational level, subjects of interest, achievement badges, study history
- **Communication Data**: Messages, support tickets, feedback, and other communications with our support team

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use Whetzy, we automatically collect:

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, browser type, unique device identifiers, device fingerprints
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features accessed, time spent, clicks, interactions, learning progress, quiz results, course completion status
- **Network Information**: IP address, referring URL, ISP information, connection type
- **Location Data**: Approximate location based on IP address (no precise GPS data collected)
- **User Agent**: Browser and application version information

### 2.3 Analytics Data

We use analytics services (including Google Analytics, if applicable) to understand how users interact with our Service.

### 2.4 Payment Information

We do NOT directly store payment card information. Payment processors: Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, RevenueCat.
