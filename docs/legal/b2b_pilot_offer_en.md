# Whetzy for Teams — Free Pilot Program

*Offer document · September 2026*

---

## What is Whetzy

Whetzy is an upskilling platform for Python, SQL, data engineering, and cloud
for technical teams. It combines an adaptive engine (BKT + FSRS) with
executable practice, verifiable certifications, and real-time progress metrics.

## The offer

We offer **3 free pilots** to companies that want to evaluate the platform
before committing. Each pilot includes:

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 90 days |
| **Seats** | Up to 25 users |
| **Access** | Full Pro Max plan (unlimited AI tutor, all tracks) |
| **Support** | Dedicated channel with the Whetzy team |
| **Cost** | $0 during the pilot |

## What we ask in return

1. **Aggregate usage data** (never individual) to improve the engine:
   - Average study time per session.
   - 30-day retention rate.
   - Most and least completed skills.
   - NPS or informal team satisfaction.

2. **A written testimonial** (2-3 sentences) that we can publish on Whetzy's
   evidence page, with company name and title of the responsible person.
   If the company prefers anonymity, we will publish only sector and size.

3. **A closing call** (30 min) at the end of the pilot to review results
   and decide whether to continue.

## After the pilot

If the team decides to continue:

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Price** | $99 per seat / year |
| **Minimum** | 10 seats |
| **Billing** | Annual, prepaid |
| **Volume discount** | Negotiable from 50 seats |

There is no lock-in: if the team does not renew at the end of the year,
accounts revert to the free plan with limited access. Certificates already
issued remain verifiable.

## How to get started

1. Write to **teams@whetzy.com** with:
   - Company name and sector.
   - Estimated number of participants.
   - Tracks of interest (Python, SQL, Data Engineering, Cloud, etc.).

2. Within 48 hours we will send credentials for participants.

3. The pilot begins when the first user logs in.

---

*Whetzy does not request personal employee data beyond corporate email and
name. Usage data is collected in aggregate. We comply with GDPR where
applicable.*
