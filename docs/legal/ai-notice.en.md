# AI Notice

## Disclaimer

This is a DRAFT document for internal review only.

## About AI-Powered Features

Whetzy uses artificial intelligence to enhance your learning experience. This notice explains how AI is used and your rights.

## What AI Does

- Generates personalized learning explanations based on your questions
- Provides context-specific tutoring for Python, SQL, and cybersecurity topics
- Helps explain complex concepts in simple terms

## What AI Cannot Do

- Access your personal information beyond your quiz responses
- Make decisions about your account, payments, or access to content
- Access other users' data or learning progress
- Store your personal information on external servers

## Model Information

- **Model**: Qwen 3.8-27B (Large Language Model)
- **Provider**: Groq (https://groq.com/)
- **Purpose**: Educational tutoring and explanation generation
- **Data Processing**: Your quiz responses are sent to Groq's servers to generate responses

## Your Privacy

- Your quiz responses are processed by Groq to generate explanations
- Whetzy does NOT train models on your data
- Groq's servers are located in the USA
- Responses are not stored by Groq; only processed for generation
- See our Privacy Policy for complete data handling information

## Limitations & Risks

AI may occasionally:
- Generate incorrect or misleading explanations
- Misunderstand your question
- Provide explanations that don't match your learning style

We recommend:
- Verifying important concepts in course materials
- Asking support@whetzy.com if explanations are unclear
- Using rule-based explanations if you prefer not to use AI

## Disable AI (Optional)

You can disable AI-powered explanations in your Settings:

1. Go to Account Settings
2. Find "AI Tutor" section
3. Toggle "Use AI explanations" to OFF
4. Whetzy will use rule-based explanations instead

## Your Rights

- **Right to Know**: You know when you're reading AI-generated content (labeled "AI-powered")
- **Right to Opt-Out**: You can disable AI mode in settings
- **Right to Accuracy**: You can flag incorrect explanations; admins will review
- **Right to Human Review**: You can request a human tutor response for any question

## Feedback

If AI explanations are incorrect or unhelpful:

1. Use the "Flag" button below the explanation
2. Provide feedback (optional)
3. Whetzy admins will review within 24 hours
4. We use this feedback to improve AI responses

## Changes to This Notice

Whetzy may update this AI Notice at any time. Users will be notified of material changes via email.
