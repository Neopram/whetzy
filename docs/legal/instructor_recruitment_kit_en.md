# Instructor Recruitment Kit — Whetzy

*September 2026 · Recruitment document*

---

## Teach what you know. Earn while others learn.

Whetzy is looking for **founding instructors** to build the first marketplace
of Python, SQL, data engineering, and cloud courses in Spanish and English
with an adaptive engine.

---

## What we offer

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Revenue share** | **80%** of each course sale (you 80%, Whetzy 20%; ADR-41) |
| **"Founding Instructor" badge** | Permanent distinction on your profile and courses |
| **Priority visibility** | Your courses appear first in the marketplace for the first 6 months |
| **Adaptive engine** | Your questions integrate with Whetzy's BKT+FSRS engine |
| **Verifiable certificates** | Students who complete your course receive an Open Badges 3.0 certificate |
| **Instructor dashboard** | Student metrics, revenue, ratings, and real-time progress |
| **Direct support** | Dedicated channel with the Whetzy team |
| **Monthly payouts** | 15th of each month, $50 minimum, via Stripe or wire transfer |

## What we ask

### Your course must include

1. **Complete lessons** in Spanish and/or English (not skeletons).
2. **>= 15 questions per skill** with at least 15% executable questions
   (where the student runs code, not just picks an option).
3. **>= 36 glossary terms** per track.
4. **Declared license** per question and lesson: original or reusable
   (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, CC-BY, CC0, PSF, public domain).
5. **Zero certification dumps** — questions copied from real exams.

Everything goes through our **automated quality gate** before manual review.

### Your profile

- Demonstrable experience in the topic (portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube).
- Commitment to keeping the course updated (at least one review per semester).

## Roadmap

| Phase | Goal | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Founders** | 5 invited instructors | Now |
| **Open** | 20 instructors with >= 1 published course | Month 3 |
| **Scale** | 50+ instructors, 100+ courses | Year 1 |

## How to apply

1. Write to **instructors@whetzy.com** with:
   - Your name and professional profile (LinkedIn, GitHub, or similar).
   - The topic you want to teach.
   - A sample: 3 example questions or a draft lesson.

2. We review your profile and sample within 72 hours.

3. If it's a match, we give you access to the instructor dashboard and
   guidance to publish your first course.

---

*Founding instructors keep their badge and priority visibility as the
program grows. The 80% rate is fixed for courses published during the
founders phase.*
